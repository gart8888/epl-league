Rules & Scoring

  • Goal: Drag and drop the 20 Premier League teams into the exact order you think they will finish at the end of the season.
  • Golf-Style Scoring: Just like golf, the lowest total score wins! You earn points based on how far off your prediction is for every team. For example, if you predict a team to finish 2nd, but they actually end up finishing 7th, you receive 5 penalty points for that pick. Spot-on predictions earn 0 points.
  • Sequential Tie-Breakers: If two or more players end up tied on total points, the system automatically breaks the tie by looking at individual picks in order:
    • The player with the more accurate 1st place prediction gets the higher rank.
    • If those are identical, it compares accuracy on 2nd place predictions, then 3rd place, and so on all the way down through 20th place.

Rules & Scoring

  • Goal: Drag and drop the 20 Premier League teams into the exact order you think they will finish at the end of the season.
  • Golf-Style Scoring: Lowest total score wins! Points represent how far off your prediction is for every team. For example, predicting a team to finish 2nd when they actually finish 7th earns 5 penalty points. Spot-on predictions earn 0 points.
  • Sequential Tie-Breakers: Ties are broken automatically behind the scenes by comparing 1st place prediction accuracy, then 2nd place, down through 20th place.

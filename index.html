Rules & Scoring System

  • Goal: Predict the final 1 to 20 standings of the Premier League table.
  • Scoring (Golf Rules): Points are awarded based on absolute position difference. For example, if you predict a team to finish 1st and they finish 5th, you receive 4 points ($|1 - 5| = 4$).
  • Lowest Score Wins: The player with the fewest total points across all 20 teams leads the standings.
  • Tie-Breaking Logic: If two or more players are tied on total points, ties are broken automatically behind the scenes:
    • The system first compares absolute error on 1st choice picks.
    • If still tied, it steps sequentially through 2nd, 3rd, 4th choice picks down through all 20 positions until the tie is broken.

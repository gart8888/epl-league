Scoring & Tie-Breaker Info

  • Golf-Style Scoring: The lowest total score wins! Points represent how far off a prediction is for each team. For example, predicting a team to finish 2nd when they actually finish 7th results in 5 penalty points. Perfect predictions earn 0 points.
  • Sequential Tie-Breakers: If two or more players end up tied on total points, ties are broken automatically behind the scenes:
    • The player with the more accurate 1st place prediction takes the higher position.
    • If those are identical, the system compares 2nd place prediction accuracy, then 3rd place, sequentially down through 20th place.
